Corked

You had an idea.
A week later, it is in your notes app,
between two others you never did anything with either.

A spark left in a notes app does not age.
It rots.

You cork an idea.
It ages.
You find out if there is something real behind it.

The cellar knows what to ask.

Corked does not give you a blank page. It asks one question at a time, shaped around the idea you corked.

If your answer is vague, the next question narrows. If the idea starts changing, the cellar follows the change. If the idea has nothing underneath it, that becomes visible too.

You do not have to plan the process. You just answer the next question.

The cellar becomes a record.

Every corked idea leaves a trace:
what it was when it arrived,
what the questions revealed,
what changed,
and whether anything survived.

Some become real.
Some are archived.
Some keep aging.

Together, they show the shape of your thinking better than a notes app ever could.
