M5 · The Existing Fix
What do people use now, and where does it fail?
M5 rule: overall state equals Gap state. A named fix with no failure point, or a failure point with no named fix, is not enough — both are required for settled.
M6 · Their Words, Not Yours
Real language, or a reconstruction?
M6 rule: overall state equals Words state. Reconstructed words cap at clearing no matter how vivid the quote. Class 2 has no Echo bar — confirm nothing crashes and no Echo chip moves for those flows.
M7 · The Non-Case
Who looks like they should have this, but doesn't?
M7 rule: overall state equals Limit state. A vague hedge ("some probably don't") is not a bounded exclusion. "Everyone has this" removes the boundary entirely.
Gap, Words, and Limit are each the only bar that earns their verdict. Everything else in that mechanism is context.
